Task Management System (TMS)

Enterprise Task & Workflow Management Platform

The Task Management System (TMS) was engineered for Ranch University to centralize institutional workflows, facilitate transparent department-level task delegation, track deadlines in real-time, and ensure accountability across internal administrative and academic units.

Built with a performant Laravel backend API and an intuitive Vue.js reactive frontend, the platform streamlines daily operations, sends instant SMS and notification triggers, and integrates directly with role-based access control (RBAC) security protocols.

Developing enterprise platforms like TMS and REDM demonstrates how technology can transform institutional management, boost organizational productivity, and deliver measurable operational impact.
